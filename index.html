About this website ↓

Behind the portfolio

Built from
the ground up.

Rather than relying on a website builder or pre-made template, I chose to develop this portfolio as a custom-coded website using HTML, CSS and JavaScript. This approach gives me greater creative freedom over its design, structure and interactivity, allowing me to create an experience that reflects my vision.

Developed in Visual Studio Code, with version control through Git and GitHub, and hosted on GitHub Pages, the website is designed to evolve alongside my learning. The portfolio is not just a record of my development in International Advertising; its design and presentation are part of that creative process.
